# Introduction
Welcome to the high-risk world of drug dealing! In this game you assume the role of a wannabe drug dealer with a couple of thousand bucks in your pocket. It is your goal to turn that small investment into as much wealth as you can in just 30 days by selling and buying drugs. Be warned though, the life of a drug dealer is a risky one; it seems everyone is out to do you harm in one way or another--the police, street gangs, loan sharks, and yes, even your own customers.

# How to Play
One you have loaded the program and clicked on the New Game button, you might be thinking to yourself, "Ok, now what?" Or you might get it right off the bat. For those of you who don't get it, here it is:

The moment you click on the New Game button, you are on day 1 of a 30 day game. In the panel on the left, you should see several drugs listed. By selecting one of these drugs and clicking on the Buy button, you can purchase it, providing you have enough money. You will notice that the panel at the right now has that drug listed.

When you have bought what you want, and feel that you can do no more, click on the Stay Here button. You are now on day 2. You will notice that the market is most likely listing different drugs than before, though some are probably the same. If you bought a drug, and it's on the list, check the price. If it is more than what you paid (the price you paid is listed in the panel in the right), you may want to consider selling it for profit. As you gain profit, you can buy more drugs than before, as well as more expensive drugs.

There is a chance that when you clicked on the Tomorrow button, something happened. Perhaps the police came after you, or you found some money, or heard a rumor. You are notified by what happens in the panel at the top. In some situations (like the police) a separate box will pop up for you to interact with.

This has been a very simplistic overview of the game, but should be enough to get you started. See below for further detail.

# Buying, Selling, and Dumping Drugs
There are many things to do in the life of a drug dealer, reflected by the options located in the central pane.

## Buy
To buy drugs, simply select the drug you are interested in. If the Buy button is gray, it means that either you can't afford the drug, or that you are carrying as much as you can carry. If the button isn't gray, you can click on it (double-clicking on the drug itself also works). You will then be asked how many you want to buy. Type in a number and click on OK. The drug will then be added to your stock.

## Sell
You can sell drugs by selecting the drug you want to sell. If the Sell button is gray, it probably means there isn't a market for the drug right now, but don't fret, the market is volatile and chances are you will be able to sell it tomorrow. If you are able to sell it, you will be asked how many that you want to sell. Fill in the number and click on OK. The drug will then be transferred from your stock into the market.

## Dump
You may sometimes need to dump some drugs. This may be because there is a super deal being offered on some other drug and you need to make room, and for some reason shipping the drug or putting in the vault isn't an option. Dumping a drug is as easy as selecting the drug you want to dump and clicking on the Dump button. You will be asked how many you want to dump, which you can enter and the confirm by clicking on OK.

# Places to Visit
There are quite a few places to visit, which you can access by clicking on the Places button.

## Finances
Here you will do your banking and dealings with loan sharks. The bank is a good place to safeguard your hard-earned money against criminals.

## Shopping
Here you can buy and sell weapons, armor, and No-Scent. Weapons have a carrying limit of one, although this limit can be exceeded if you find a weapon somewhere. The same thing goes for No-Scent, although the limit that you may carry is ten. Armor is used to protect you from bodily harm. It is highly recommended that you aquire some as soon as possible because it can mean the difference between becoming a drug lord or dying an early death at the hands of some ruffians.

## Hospital
You are going to get hurt. Small scrapes and bruises will heal themselves in a couple of days, but other injuries will take much longer to heal, leaving you at risk. The hospital will heal your wounds for a price. If you walk in with only a broken bone or a gunshot wound, they can patch you up fairly cheap. However, if you walk in and you are near death, you better have a small fortune at your disposal because such repairs are costly. The best advice is to treat your wounds immediately after a fight so they don't compound.

## The Vault
In every city, you have a remotely located vault that you can use for storing any amount of drugs you wish. Because the vault is out of the way, you can only access it once per day. 

## Shipping
While you can certainally spray your drugs with No-Scent, and bring them on a plane with you, it is still risky to do so. There is always a small chance that the dogs will smell something no matter how much No-Scent you use. The last thing you need is all of airport security coming down on you. To ensure your longevity, you might want to use a shipper. They will ship drugs to the city of your choice with no possible risk of bodily harm to you. They aren't always reliable, however. The lower priced ones tend to lose shipments and are watched more closely by the police. The higher priced onces are concerned about their image and will do everything humanly possible to make sure you receive your shipment.

# Information
You have at your disposal tools that represent your knowledge of the drug world and connections within it. These tools are accessed by clicking on the Info... button.

## Vaults
You keep detailed notes of what cities you are storing things in.

## World Drug Prices and World Cities
By using your contacts, you know what the going price for a drug is anywhere in the world.

## Shipment Status
Check on the progress of your shipment. 

## History
Pops up a window that displays the history of your health, cash, debt, and the drug price history for all the cities. Useful for noting trends.

# Tomorrow
When you feel that you have done as much as can be done, it's time to get some sleep and wait for tomorrow to come.

## Stay Here
If you want to stay in the city that you are currently in, click on the Stay Here button.

## Fly Away
Whether you have heard a rumor about a super deal in some far corner of the world, or they simply have better prices there, you may want to take a trip. Flying with drugs is relatively safe provided you spray them with No-Scent first to avoid detection by the dogs. Note that you can carry only ten cans of No-Scent, which makes travelling with more than 1000 units of drugs risky. To avoid this risk, you can use a shipper to move the drug where you want it to be, and the hop aboard a plane to catch up with it. A word of caution though, sometimes shipments are delayed or going missing. The only way you can be sure that a drug will be there when you are is to carry it with you.

For the purpose of gameplay, you are not limited in carrying weapons onboard a plane. In real life, you can be sure that your flame-thrower would be noticed.

# General Game Information

## The Market
The market is where your will be buying drugs from and selling drugs to. Not all days are the same though. Some days drugs are plentiful and buyers and sellers for virtually every drug can be found. On other days, supplies might be scarce, or the market might not be receptive to what you're selling. On rare occasions, the market won't have a certain drug for sale, but is interested in buying it.

## Ranks
You can have one of six ranks at any give time:

### Wannabe
Cash Required: $0
Carrying Capacity: 10 units
Container: pants pocket

### Small-time Operator
Cash Required: $5,000
Carrying Capacity: 25 units
Container: coat

### Dealer
Cash Required: $40,000
Carrying Capacity: 100 units
Container: black Trans Am

### Big-Time Dealer
Cash Required: $300,000
Carrying Capacity: 600 units
Container: converted milk truck

### Distributer
Cash Required: $2,500,000
Carrying Capacity: 3,500 units
Container: warehouse

### Drug Lord
Cash Required: $15,000,000
Carrying Capacity: 20,000 units
Container: island paradise

You start off as a wannabe, but change rank to small-time operator when you get $5,000 and hold it for at least three days. The same goes for the other ranks, but the point at which you advance is different for each rank. See the table above. When--and if--you manage to accumulate 15 million dollars, and keep it for three days, the title of Drug Lord will be given to you.

If a deal goes bad on you and you find that you've lost a lot of money, it's quite possible that you will be demoted to a lower rank. The wise quickly learn never to spend so much that it will put them at risk.

Your rank dictates how much goods you can carry. As a wannabe, you only have your pants pockets available to you, and can't carry very much--only five units. As you progress in rank, you can carry more and more--for good reason: there are more people willing to sell and buy from you as your reputation grows.

For each up in rank, you also gain five extra days of gameplay.

## Finances
There are two financial institutions that you can deal with: banks and loan sharks. Banks offer complete protection for your money and while it is in the bank, it can't be stolen or robbed from. It is always a good idea to keep some cash in the bank to cover yourself.

Loan sharks aren't quite as reputable. They will lend you some money for a few days at extraordinarily high interest rates. If you don't pay it back on time, they will come looking for you and won't be polite about it.

There are different loan sharks available in the game. They each offer a certain amount of money based upon the cash you have on hand, as long as it doesn't exceed your credit limit, which depends on your rank. For example, Laughing Max makes you a loan that is 3 times the cash you have on you. If you have $500 cash in your pocket, he will add $1,500 to it. Unfortunately, he charges 50% interest compounded daily on it, so it's in your best interest to pay him off as early as possible (there's a small fee for early payments). If you don't pay him in time, he will seek you out. After a few days of talks, he will start to get rough.

## Shopping
Make no mistake about it, drug dealing is a risky business. Seems that someone is always shooting at you. Only makes sense that you should be shooting back.

The store has an assortment of weapons available for you to use on those who would seek to harm you. Some, like the knife, are cheap, but are likely to miss and when they do hit only affect one person at a time. Others, like dynamite, usually hit and effect entire crowds at once. Good thing too, because as you escalate in rank you will find that they like to send a whole lot of police after you.

Some rudimentary armor is also available. Items like the leather jacket and bullet-proof vest go a long way in increasing your lifespan.

## Your Status
Your status is shown in the panel in the bottom right of the main window. All of the fields are self-explanatory, except for the box in the lower right. This is a history view of different things: your cash, your health, your debt, as well as the market values of drugs. Clicking on it advances to the next item. Right clicking pops up a menu that lets you go to an item directly, or zoom in on the current data. For drugs, the dotted gray line represents the average price, and the solid gray lines represent its normal price range. 

## Fights
Gun battles are the norm for drug dealers. When you find yourself in a fight, the fight dialog box will pop up. In some cases you can try to surrender or bribe whoever is after you. In all cases you can try to run. If you have a weapon--and ammo-- you can try to fight them. Large groups can quickly overtake you though, so be sure you have the right kind of firepower and armor.
